# Mira's Clinic

## Location
Mira's clinic is beside the western bridge in Moonlight Village.

## Visiting Hours
Mira receives visitors at her clinic each morning from 8 to 11.

## Services
Mira treats patients and prepares herbal remedies at the clinic.

## Supplies
Mira's supply of healing herbs is running low.
Villagers can help by delivering healing herbs to her.
